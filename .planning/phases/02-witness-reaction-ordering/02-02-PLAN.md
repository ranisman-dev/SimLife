<objective>
Two behavior-preserving refactors that make urgency ordering possible at all: move the gossip
candidate's two RNG rolls out of candidate construction into a lazily-evaluated `resolve()`
hook, then extract the candidate-scoring body of `decideAndAct()` into a pure
`scoreCandidates()` (CONTEXT.md D-01).

Purpose: Plan 02-03 scores every witness *before* dispatching any of them, and `decideAndAct`
scores again at real dispatch time (D-07, resolved in 02-03). `scoreCandidates()` therefore
runs twice per reacting witness. Today the gossip branch draws from `world.rng` during
candidate construction, so calling it twice would double-consume the RNG stream and silently
change which claim gets told and to whom between the two passes. Deferring those rolls is what
makes the ordering pass safe; the extraction is what makes the scorer reusable.

Output: `scoreCandidates(world, witness, event, appraisal, priorRelationship)` returning
`{ reacts, candidates }`, exported alongside `appraiseEvent`; `decideAndAct` reduced to
"score, pick, resolve, log, fire".
</objective>
