<objective>
Close MEMORY-01's second half and MEMORY-02: give a `Move` that is causally a flight from
violence a real impact, and raise the memory-importance ceiling so genuinely severe memories
outlive the existing ~38-tick halflife / ~190-tick prune-crossover cap.

Purpose: `Move` falls through `appraiseEvent`'s branch chain with `impact` at `0`, so every
repositioning — including visibly fleeing in the immediate aftermath of being attacked — forms
a memory at the `0.1` floor. Separately, `perceiveEvent`'s
`clamp(Math.abs(appraisal.impact), 0.1, 1)` (`sim.js:720`) has been silently clamping the
most severe witnessed events for the whole life of the project: a direct `Attack` victim
appraises at exactly `-1.2` for every fixture NPC (`isVictim` skips the damping block), so
every victim memory in the codebase has been stored at `1`. Raising the bound is a strict
widening of an existing clamp, not a new mechanism.

**Two consequences to expect, not prevent.** (1) Like Plan 04-01's Tell change, a non-zero
Move impact means `applyAppraisal` (`sim.js:804`) now runs for flight Moves where it
previously hit its `impact === 0` early exit — the witness's `grievance` toward the *fleeing
mover* rises and an `Indignation` emotion is pushed. That is a direct consequence of D-04's
locked negative sign; record it, do not add a special case to suppress it. (2) Every direct
`Attack` victim's stored `importance` moves from `1` to `1.2` (or higher for a victim holding
a positive `Safety` value, up to the new `1.5` ceiling). That is the single largest predicted
golden-master diff of this phase and is a MECHANISM this phase causes, not a regression.

Output: `appraiseEvent`'s `Move` branch, `TUNING.maxMemoryImportance`, the one-line clamp
change, and recorded per-witness flight-impact numbers.
</objective>
