<objective>
Give `Tell` events a real, severity-scaled `appraiseEvent` impact so being told something
explosive forms a memory as important as witnessing it, instead of every Tell landing at the
flat `0.1` floor.

Purpose: MEMORY-01's first half. `appraiseEvent` (`sim.js:779-802`) has branches for `Take`,
`Attack` and `Give` and none for `Tell`, so `impact` stays `0` and
`clamp(Math.abs(0), 0.1, 1)` makes "Mara is dead" and "Mara took some bread" form identically
weak memories. This plan closes that for Tell; Plan 04-02 closes it for Move and raises the
ceiling.

**Blast radius you must expect and record, not prevent.** Today every Tell reaches
`applyAppraisal` (`sim.js:804`) with `impact === 0` and hits its `if (impact === 0) return;`
early exit. After this plan, Tell impacts are non-zero, so `applyAppraisal` runs for the first
time on Tell events. For a witness told directly (`isVictim === true`) that means, **against
the teller**: `trust` down, `affection` down, `grievance` up, an `Anger` emotion pushed, and —
via the `if (appraisal.isVictim)` guard at `sim.js:822` — a `SeekRestitution` goal opened
against the messenger. Trust then feeds back into the very confidence formula this plan
duplicates, and grievance/anger feed `scoreCandidates`. A Tell can now cause the listener to
attack the teller. This is a direct consequence of D-06's locked sign term (which exists
*because* `applyAppraisal` branches on sign) and is in scope. Do NOT invent a seam that splits
memory importance from appraisal impact — `appraiseEvent` has one return value consumed by
both paths by design, and splitting it would be a scope reduction of a locked decision. Task 2
exists to trace and record exactly what this cascade does, so Plan 04-03 can regression-verify
it and Plan 04-04 can classify it in the baseline diff and document it.

Output: `TELL_SEVERITY`, `appraiseEvent`'s `Tell` branch, and a recorded numeric + cascade
trace for every fixture witness.
</objective>
