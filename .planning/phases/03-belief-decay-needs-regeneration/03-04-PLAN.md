<objective>
Replace the single `safety < 0.7` retreat gate with a two-threshold hysteresis band: a
witness starts retreating for safety reasons below `0.65` and stops being gated in by safety
only above `0.75`, so a value drifting through the band cannot flicker the decision every
tick.

Purpose: DECAY-05 and ROADMAP Phase 3 success criterion 5. Passive regeneration (Plan 03-02)
makes `safety` a continuously moving value for the first time, which is precisely what turns
a single-threshold gate into a flicker source.

**Purity is the load-bearing constraint here.** Phase 2 made `scoreCandidates()` a pure,
twice-called scorer: `orderWitnesses()` calls it once per witness in a read-only ranking
pre-pass, and `decideAndAct()` calls it again at real dispatch (02-03-SUMMARY.md, "Next Phase
Readiness"). The invariant Phase 2 actually bought is **`scoreCandidates` writes nothing**,
with dispatch recomputing fresh — NOT "the pre-pass and dispatch observe identical state",
which is false in general: a reaction cascade (`MAX_REACTION_DEPTH` 4) can append to a later
witness's log between the pre-pass and that witness's dispatch. So the hysteresis state must
be READ inside `scoreCandidates` and WRITTEN only from `decideAndAct`, where the two existing
log writes already live. A write inside `scoreCandidates` would execute during the ordering
pre-pass and corrupt dispatch ranking; a write in `decideAndAct` cannot, because the pre-pass
never calls it.

**The marker must discriminate WHY retreat won.** The retreat gate is a three-way disjunction
(`rel.fear > 0.3 || <safety condition> || fearEmotion > 0.2`), so a retreat candidate can be
scored — and win — with safety at 0.95 purely on fear. A bare "the last entry that mentions
`retreat=` chose retreat" scan cannot tell that apart from a safety-driven retreat, and would
latch a fear-driven witness onto the looser Exit threshold (0.75) for subsequent safety-only
scoring — directly contradicting D-07's "retreating **for safety reasons**". The mechanism
below therefore carries a machine-readable `safetyDriven` flag on the retreat candidate, which
`decideAndAct` records as `retreatForSafety` on the winner's log entry; the scan reads that
marker, not candidate-label presence.

Output: two `TUNING` thresholds, the `safetyDriven`/`retreatForSafety` marker path, a
read-only `isCurrentlyRetreating()` helper, the banded gate, and four DECAY-05 checks driven
by a scripted oscillation plus a fear-driven discrimination case.
</objective>
