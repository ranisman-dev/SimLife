records read through a live accessor that regenerates them asymptotically toward `1`, and
fix every read site — including the two in `presentation.js` that would otherwise throw.

subtracts. This plan is the structural half; `belonging`'s trigger (Plan 03-03) and the
retreat hysteresis (Plan 03-04) both build on the accessor introduced here.

   `TypeError: v.toFixed is not a function` the first time the mind inspector or debug
   report opens after this lands. Fixing them is part of this plan, not a follow-up.

Output: `NEED_DEFAULTS`, `needValue()`, a tick-threaded `adjustNeed`, both `sim.js` read
sites swapped, `presentation.js` reading through the public API, and DECAY-03 checks
including an explicit purity assertion.
