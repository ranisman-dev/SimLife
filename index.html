Tiny Town

A prototype for a "beliefs, not scripts" simulation architecture: 5 NPCs, one location, five generic verbs (Take, Give, Attack, Tell, Move). Every action — yours or theirs — runs through the same perceive → believe → decide → act pipeline. Nothing here is scripted per-scenario.
